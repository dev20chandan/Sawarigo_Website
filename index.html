Sawarigo is a modern ride-booking platform built to make everyday travel simple, safe, affordable, and reliable. Whether you need a Bike, Auto, or Car, Sawarigo helps you find the right ride for your journey with just a few taps.

We believe travelling should be stress-free and accessible to everyone. Our platform connects passengers with trusted drivers while focusing on fair pricing, quick bookings, transparent service, and passenger safety.

HOW IT WORKS

Your Ride, Just a Few Simple Steps

Sawarigo makes booking and driving a ride quick, simple, and rewarding.

1
Choose Your Ride

Select the ride that fits your needs — Bike, Auto, or Car.

2
Enter Your Location

Enter your pickup location and destination to find available rides near you.

3
Book Your Ride

Check the ride details and fare, then confirm your booking with just a tap.

4
Meet Your Driver

Get your driver's details and track your ride in real time while your driver comes to pick you up.

5
Enjoy Your Journey

Sit back and enjoy a safe, comfortable, and reliable ride to your destination.

6
Ride & Pay

Complete your journey and pay conveniently through the available payment options.

1
Download & Register

Install the Sawarigo Captain App and complete your quick KYC registration.

2
Go Online

Toggle your status to online anytime you are ready to start receiving ride requests.

3
Accept Rides

Review ride requests and fare details, then tap to accept the trips you want.

4
Pick Up Passenger

Navigate to the pickup location using built-in GPS and meet your rider safely.

5
Complete Journey

Drop the rider at their destination following the designated route map.

6
Earn & Grow

Collect your earnings instantly and enjoy exclusive driver rewards and low commissions!
